# Roadmap Stage Functionality Decisions

Manager authoring guidance for the active planning contract owners. This is not
an intermediate approval gate; follow the planning workflow's complete draft.

1. Map each included capability to a current outcome, consumer, supported path,
   or demonstrated failure. Keep requirements concrete and dependency-aware.
2. Record defaults, failures, unsupported behavior, durable/replay consequences,
   and explicit deferrals at each requirement's owner.
3. Resolve repository-backed defaults locally. Raise material unresolved product
   choices early with recommendations, evidence, tradeoffs, and exact feedback.
   Batch independent related questions; handle dependent decisions sequentially.
4. Update the affected requirements and decision rows after answers. Continue
   independent design/validation drafting while a decision is pending, marking
   dependent content provisional. Unresolved material choices block readiness,
   not all useful drafting.

Do not seek another approval for a decision already covered by the user's
accepted scope. The complete packet receives final review and approval.
